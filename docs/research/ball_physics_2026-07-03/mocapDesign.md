## 1. BALL RIGID BODY — FEASIBILITY

**Marker choice: retroreflective tape patches only; hemispherical markers are disqualified for bounce/strike trials.** A 3–4 mm hemispherical marker protrudes 2–4 mm from a 40 mm ball: it WILL be the contact point on some bounces, invalidating the very contact physics being measured, and 4 stalk markers weigh ~0.4–0.8 g (+15–30%). Tape patches (~0.1–0.2 mm thick retro film, ~200–300 g/m² — JUDGMENT, typical vendor values, weigh to confirm): a 10 mm circle ≈ 0.016–0.024 g; **8–12 patches ≈ 0.13–0.3 g total = +5–11% mass**. Budget: total added mass ≤ 0.3 g, weighed on a 0.01 g scale before/after taping, recorded in metadata.

**Quantified effect of added mass (the +0.5 g case):** m: 2.7→3.2 g (+18.5%). k_d and k_m are *acceleration* coefficients (force/m), so at equal Cd/Cm the markered ball reads **−15.6%** on both (×2.7/3.2). Mass ratio is exactly measurable → correct multiplicatively: k_clean = k_markered × (m_markered/2.7 g) × (C_clean/C_markered). The residual is the surface-roughness Cd shift, which the v0 data bounds at roughly +10–25% (0.136 markered vs 0.107–0.122 clean-ish) — this is why total tape area must be minimized and why one **clean-vs-taped A/B free-flight set** (Sec. 5) is required. Contact model: the spin-equation is written in velocity/impulse-per-mass form with inertia entering only via c = I/(mR²) (yaml contact comment; `physics/params.py` ContactParams); uniformly distributed surface tape preserves c ≈ 2/3, so **fitted e_eff/a_t/b_t transfer to the clean ball without mass correction** (JUDGMENT, holds to first order). Restitution perturbation from tape at the contact patch: measure directly via A/B drop tests.

**6-DoF solvability on a spinning ball at 300 Hz — mostly NO.** Rotation per frame: **30 rev/s → 36°/frame; 60 rev/s → 72°/frame; 100 rev/s → 120°/frame**. An asymmetric pattern (≥4 simultaneously visible, non-coplanar-on-sphere patches with all pairwise angular distances distinct) makes each single frame's orientation unique in principle, but frame-to-frame marker labeling in incremental rigid-body solvers breaks when per-frame rotation approaches half the minimum inter-marker angular gap (~60–70° gaps with 8 patches → break at ~30–35°/frame), and self-occlusion swaps the visible patch set every 2–3 frames at 36°/frame. JUDGMENT: usable ≤ ~20 rev/s, marginal 20–40 rev/s, hopeless ≥ 60 rev/s; absolute ceiling 150 rev/s (180°/frame) even with perfect labeling. Whether CMTracker's solver is incremental or per-frame-global, and its minimum patch size at venue camera distance, **could not be confirmed from the repo** (FLAG — test empirically day 1: spin the taped ball by hand in front of the cameras, watch for orientation flips in `/vrpn_mocap/Ball/pose`).

**Primary spin path (recommendation): position-only + physics inversion — v0 already validated this.** (a) In-flight: fit ω (2 observable components ⊥ v) per segment by RK4 shooting with k_m frozen at 0.0042; lateral Magnus deflection Δ ≈ ½k_m·ω·v·t² = 17 mm per 10 rad/s at v=4 m/s, t=0.4 s, vs ~1 mm mocap noise → resolution a few rad/s (<1 rev/s). The along-velocity (rifle) component is unobservable (ω×v=0) — design tosses so the spin of interest is ⊥ v (top/back/side all are). Topspin channel is g-degenerate per the v0 fit — resolve by freezing g=9.81 and k_d from no-spin trials, and weight the lateral channel. (b) Across a bounce: with the grippy table model known, (v−, v+) inversion recovers the tangential ω− components. (c) Ground truth spot-check: phone super-slow-mo (240–960 fps) on a half-blackened spare ball for ~10 trials/session; 60 rev/s at 240 fps is 90°/frame — use 960 fps mode if available (Ace's event-camera logo tracking is the pro-grade version of this, Methods line 1089).

**CMTracker config:** ball as named rigid body `Ball`, relay `ball_tracking_mode:=rigid_body ball_object:=Ball` (avatar_pro_vrpn.yaml:26-31); even if 6-DoF orientation is garbage at spin, the rigid-body *position* solve (centroid) is fine and preferred over `auto`.

## 2. RACKET RIGID BODY

- **4–5 markers (9.5–12 mm spheres are fine here), asymmetric, on the frame edge + handle butt — never on rubber.** Keep them out of the blade's ball-approach corridor (top edge + handle side).
- **Face-normal + face-center calibration** (once per session, 2 min): lay the racket flat on the table (table plane verified z=0 via `PPT`/corner capture, frames doc:9-12), record 10 s static, both faces up. Face normal in body frame = R_bodyᵀ·ẑ_world; averaging the two sides also yields the blade-thickness offset. Face center: one extra static capture with a temporary marker taped at the face center (remove afterwards). Cross-check against the FK mount definition later (`A3_MOUNT_OFFSET`, normal = racket-local +Y, frames doc:89-93) — but for physics fitting the mocap body frame is self-contained.
- **Occlusion around contact (±30 ms = ±9 frames at 300 Hz):** contact dwell is ~1–3 ms (sub-frame). The model needs racket pose + contact-point velocity AT contact. Bridge: fit a constant-acceleration (or cubic-spline) model to the racket 6-DoF over [−120, −15] ms and extrapolate; at light-stroke accelerations (≤50 m/s²) extrapolation error over 30 ms is ≤ ~0.02 m/0.15 m/s (JUDGMENT). Do NOT use post-contact frames near impact (blade vibration). Validate the bridge on unoccluded swings by artificially masking ±30 ms: require <5 mm, <0.15 m/s, <2° vs truth. Contact time itself: intersect pre/post ball-trajectory shooting fits (resolution ~1–3 ms); refine by minimizing the contact-model residual over t_c.
- Exclusion windows: drop ball samples ±5 frames (±17 ms) and racket samples ±2 frames around contact (2D marker-merge risk when ball and racket markers overlap in camera views).

## 3. TRIAL MATRIX (low-speed: 2–8 m/s, 0–60 rev/s)

Statistical basis: each free-flight trial yields ~120–190 position samples (0.4–0.6 s at 300 Hz), so per-trial parameter precision is noise-limited at <1–2%; trial counts are set by **between-trial scatter** (rig distortion, ball orientation, release variation — v0's cross-rig k_d spread was ±12%) and by **domain coverage** of (incidence θ, |u_t|/|u_n|, v_z). Rule used: n per cell ≥ 10 → cell-mean SE ≈ σ_trial/√10 ≈ 3–5% for σ_trial ≈ 10–15%; scalar-per-event parameters (e_eff) follow the v0 precedent of ~100 events (101-bounce recal → median SE ≈ 1.253σ/√n ≈ 0.0025 for σ≈0.02).

## 4. FITTING + HELD-OUT VALIDATION

**Parameter → subset assignment (in order, each stage freezing the previous):** g frozen at 9.81 (yaml precedent — rig readings 9.76–9.82 are artifacts); k_d ← Set A; k_m ← Set B lateral channel (accept only if inside the OptiTrack CI [0.0035, 0.0049], else suspect rig scale/tilt); table e_eff(v_z) ← C1; table a_t/b_t/μ ← C2+C3; paddle e_eff/a_t/b_t/μ ← D (with per-trial racket state measured, ball ω⁻ from pre-contact flight fit).

**Robust fitting:** segment at contacts by impulse detection (|Δv| between shooting-fit windows); exclusion ±3 frames (10 ms) table / ±5 frames (17 ms) racket around each contact. **RK4 shooting per segment** (free: p₀,v₀,ω₀; shared: physics params) with Huber loss on positions — NOT finite-difference acceleration: double-differencing 1 mm noise at 300 Hz gives σ_a ~O(10²) m/s² vs a 3–4 m/s² drag/Magnus signal (drag accel at 5 m/s = k_d·v² = 3.05 m/s²; Magnus at 30 rev/s, 5 m/s = 3.96 m/s² — both well-observable via shooting over ≥0.3 s, invisible in per-frame accel). Leave-one-trial-out for parameter scatter; timestamps from VRPN header stamps, verified by a racket-taps-static-ball coincidence event per session.

**Held-out protocol:** stratified 70/30 split **by trial** within every cell; fit on 70, report on 30. Acceptance thresholds (tied to the reward use-case; table 2.74×1.525 m, opponent half 1.37 m deep, net 0.1525 m — frames doc:9-16):
1. **Free flight:** propagate from the first 60 ms of state; position error at +0.4 s ≤ 25 mm median / 60 mm p95 (matches v0's 22 mm landing-median precedent).
2. **Bounce:** outgoing speed ≤5%, direction ≤3°, next-apex height ≤10 mm; ω⁺ ≤15% where spin is observable.
3. **Strike → landing (the money metric):** from measured pre-contact racket state + incoming ball state, predict the first-bounce landing point: **median ≤ 0.10 m, p90 ≤ 0.25 m, on/off-table classification ≥ 90%**.

Threshold justification (JUDGMENT): the reward uses (a) binary lands-in-opponent-half and (b) landing-target shaping (`landing_in_opponent_half` reward + `ball_predicted_landing` critic obs already in v0, docs/ball_physics.md Isaac section; P2.5-lite "PACE at-contact landing reward", docs/NOW.md:99). A policy's landing dispersion mid-training is ≥0.3 m; ranking by predicted land-rate is preserved when model error ≤ ~σ_policy/3 ≈ 0.10 m, since only shots within ±0.10 m of a table edge (≲10–15% of a centered σ=0.4 m distribution over a 1.37 m half) can be misclassified, symmetrically across policies. 0.10 m is also 7% of the half-length — small vs the 0.2–0.3 m target tolerance any landing-target reward will use, so shaping gradients stay correct. Additionally require vertical error ≤ 30 mm at the net plane (net is 0.1525 m — net-clearance scoring). If (3) fails but (1)+(2) pass, the deficit is the paddle model — expected, it has never been venue-fit — iterate on Set D only, and consider Ace's two structural upgrades: e(v_z) and an explicit slip branch (Ace Methods eq. 4–6).
