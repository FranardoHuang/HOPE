## C. Magnus-curvature-fit fallback at low speed

- **CORRECTED (arithmetic)** — Δ = ½·k_m·ω·v·t² = ½·0.0042·10·4·0.16 = **13.4 mm**, not 17 mm (formula right, number ~27% high; conclusion vs ~1 mm noise unchanged).
- **CONFIRMED (noise floor)** — per-trial OLS quadratic fit, N≈120 samples over T=0.4 s at σ=1 mm gives σ_accel ≈ 0.015 m/s² → σ_ω ≈ 0.9 rad/s at v=4 m/s. "Few rad/s, <1 rev/s" noise resolution is right.
- **CORRECTED (systematics understated)** — every inverted ω inherits the **k_m 95% CI of ±17% as a pure scale error** (yaml CI [0.0035, 0.0049]); rig tilt 0.15° leaks g·sin(0.15°)=0.026 m/s² into the lateral channel → ~1.5 rad/s bias at v=4; a 10% k_d error corrupts the (agent-proposed, g-frozen) topspin channel by ~2 rev/s. Systematic floor ≈ 2–4 rad/s, not the noise floor.
- **CORRECTED (the big one: spin-ratio saturation makes the fallback unsound over most of the target grid)**. FACTS: v0's a = k_m(ω×v) with constant k_m implies lift coefficient C_L = 2·m·k_m·ω/(ρπr²v)·(1) = **0.75·S** where S = rω/v (derived from yaml constants: 2·0.0027·0.0042/(1.2·π·4e-4·0.02) = 0.752). Ace's cM = 0.1·v/(rω) − 0.001 with force ∝ cM·r³·ω·v implies Magnus force ≈ ω-INDEPENDENT, C_L ≈ 0.27 constant (their (4/3)πρr³ prefactor, Methods eq. (1)) — a fully saturated model fit in the pro regime. The two models cross at **S* ≈ 0.36**. JUDGMENT: sports-ball aerodynamics generically show C_L linear in S only for small S, plateauing ~0.3–0.5; both in-hand models are consistent with that (v0 = the linear branch, Ace = the plateau). Now the planned regime: S = 0.02·ω/v; at v=4 m/s, 10 rev/s → S=0.31, 30 rev/s → S=0.94, 60 rev/s → S=1.9. **Most of trial-set B's 10–60 rev/s grid sits at or above the plateau knee.** Consequence: if C_L saturates, trajectory curvature loses sensitivity to |ω| — under Ace's model the inversion returns ω_est ≈ C_L·ρπr²v²/(2·m·k_m·v) ≈ 17.8·v rad/s (≈11 rev/s at v=4) *no matter whether true spin is 20 or 60 rev/s*. The fallback is therefore sound as a **spin-magnitude** sensor only for S ≲ 0.35 (ω ≲ 17.5·v rad/s: ≲11 rev/s at 4 m/s, ≲22 rev/s at 8 m/s), and degrades to an **axis-direction-only** sensor above S ≈ 0.5. It also means v0's constant k_m=0.0042 is an effective average over whatever S-range qinghua's throws spanned, and extrapolates in neither direction.

- **CONFIRMED (observability structure)** — ω∥v produces no Magnus (unobservable in flight); across a bounce with n=ẑ, ω×n = (ω_y, −ω_x, 0) so the two horizontal components enter the tangential impulse and ω_z (about the table normal) is unobservable by the v0 contact model (no torsional term, `spin_contact.py` s(θ) form). ## D. Bottom line

1. **Rigid-body mocap spin at 300 Hz: CONFIRMED infeasible as the primary sensor above ~20–30 rev/s** (labeling aliasing at Δ_min/2 ≈ 32–37°/frame for 8 patches, compounded by motion blur ≥1 mm and 2–4 px patch images that the agent did not count; 3–5 markers make solvability worse, not better). Below ~15–20 rev/s it may work — worth the day-1 empirical test, which is also the only way to settle the UNVERIFIABLE CMTracker-solver question.
2. **Magnus-curvature fallback: PARTIALLY SOUND ONLY.** Noise-wise excellent (~1 rad/s), but (a) ±17% k_m scale uncertainty, (b) 2 of 3 components, and critically (c) probable C_L(S) saturation above S≈0.35–0.5 collapses magnitude sensitivity exactly where the low-speed grid lives (10–60 rev/s at 2–8 m/s ⇒ S up to ~1.9). The v0-vs-Ace model disagreement (0.75·S vs 0.27 constant, crossover S≈0.36) is itself repo+paper FACT; which is right in the low-speed regime is the single most valuable question the new data can answer — but only with independent spin ground truth (dotted ball + high-fps), which must be promoted from spot-check to core calibration.
