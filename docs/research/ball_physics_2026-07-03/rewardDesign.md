## F3. Papers
- **PACE** (2509.21690v3, §IV-B "Returning reward", p.4 of extracted text): at the moment of contact, use simulation physics to predict landing (x̂,ŷ) and penalize distance to desired landing on opponent side + reward net-crossing height at a specified margin; rationale = replaces late/sparse outcome signal with immediate at-contact feedback. Hit-guidance rewards are clamped inside a tolerance and disabled near flight end to preserve strike-strategy freedom (§IV-B.1).
- **Ace** (s41586-026-10338-5, Methods "Rewards"/"States"): terminal-state rewards categorized miss / hit-no-return / return; a subset of policies use a **parameterized return reward** — desired y-landing `ydesired` + weights `wreward=[wp, ws]`, wp∈[0,1] weights landing-distance, **ws∈[−1,1] weights a term proportional to the ball's y-axis angular velocity ON LANDING** — sampling these yields aiming/topspin/backspin behaviors; plus event tables (high-top-spin-return buffer) and hindsight replay setting ydesired:=achieved. This is the direct precedent for our spin-produced reward.

# DESIGN (JUDGMENT unless marked otherwise)

## TIER 1 — virtual-ball reward inside the existing racket-target task (no ball asset, no obs change, 175-D contract untouched)

**Mechanism.** Three insertions, all following existing patterns:
1. *Per-swing sampling* (in `_resample_command`, hope_commands.py:705–775): sample a virtual incoming ball `(v_in, ω_in)` per env — position at strike is `racket_target_pos_w` **by construction** (the ball is defined to arrive at the commanded strike point at the strike time). Draw `v_in` from the venue-fitted incoming-ball distribution (interim: back out from `ServeConfig.reachable_returner`, geometry.py:122–129, evolved to the mocap fit yikang is collecting now); `ω_in` from the low-speed regime (0–60 rev/s). Store as command-term buffers.
2. *At-contact evaluation* (in `_update_metrics`, next to the `exact_strike` block, hope_commands.py:1031–1074): on `exact_strike`, run `predict_contact(v_in, racket_lin_vel_w, racket_normal_w, ω_in, params.paddle)` on the **achieved** FK state (all inputs already live: lines 777–798), then a *coarse* landing rollout (below) → cache `virtual_landing_xy/valid/net_z/net_valid/omega_out` buffers, mirroring v0's `_predicted_*` caches (table_tennis_env.py:92–97). **Capture gate**: only evaluate if `pos_err < 0.095 m` (racket 0.075 + ball 0.020, the same radius the v0 env uses for real hits, table_tennis_env.py:31–33) — a whiff scores 0 for all virtual terms.
3. *Reward terms* (new functions in `mdp/hope_rewards.py`, same `_cmd(env,…)` buffer-read pattern as lines 45–52), one-shot on the exact-strike step:

| term | formula | weight (start) | rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| `virtual_pass_net` | `exp(−(net_z−(h_net+0.12))²/(2·0.10²)) + 0.5·1[net_z>h_net+R]`, ×capture gate | **20** | port of v0 `pass_net_margin` (rewards.py:77–103, v0 weight 50); clear-net-first ordering above landing per PACE/v0 comment |
| `virtual_landing` | `1[net cleared]·exp(−‖xy−xy_tgt‖²/0.3²) + 1.0·1[on_opponent ∧ net cleared]`, ×gate | **30** | port of v0 `landing_in_opponent_half` (rewards.py:34–74) incl. its anti-net-farming gate (lines 52–57). Fixed `xy_tgt` per swing type ⇒ no obs change. Integrated per-swing magnitude ≈ the current racket_position term (4.0 × ~0.5 kernel × ~10 window steps ≈ 20) — same order, deliberately dominant |
| `virtual_spin` | `clamp(ω_out·(ẑ×d̂_xy)/ω_ref, 0, 1)`, ω_ref≈250 rad/s (~40 rev/s), **×1[landing valid]** ×gate | **5 → 10 ramp** | Ace's ws-term (Methods "Rewards": reward ∝ ball y-axis angular velocity on landing), projected on the topspin axis of the outgoing direction; gating on a legal landing prevents farming spin with wild swipes that miss the table |
| reweights | `racket_velocity` 2.0→**0.5**, `racket_normal` 2.0→**0.5**; keep `racket_position` 4.0, `racket_strike_success` 5.0, `racket_progress` 10.0 unchanged | | vel/normal become shaping, not the task |

**Compute budget (FACT-anchored estimate).** Do NOT call the shipped `predict_landing`: write a fixed-length, branch-free coarse variant for the reward path — RK4 h=10 ms, 60–100 steps (0.6–1.0 s horizon covers 2–8 m/s low-speed shots), crossing by linear/quadratic interpolation instead of 24-iter bisection (interp error at h=10 ms ≪ the σ=0.3 landing kernel). Measured: 27–45 ms/step → +0.65–1.1 s/iter = **+12–23%** on the current 4.7–5.3 s/iter, eager. Because it's launch-bound and now branch-free, `torch.compile(mode="reduce-overhead")`/CUDA-graph it → expect ~5–10× less overhead, i.e. **+2–5% final** (estimate). RK4 truncation at h=10 ms is O(h⁴), negligible for smooth drag+Magnus; verify h-choice against the branch's numpy-oracle tests (`test_ball_physics_vs_record.py`) before training. Keep the shipped fine predictor for tests/offline.

**Does this subsume the commanded-velocity gate / fix vel-conditioning? (JUDGMENT)** Largely yes, and that is the main strategic payoff. The 0.5 m/s vector-matching gate (cfg:1394, pass logic 1071–1074) demands *one specific* racket velocity; the contact model maps a whole manifold of (velocity, normal, timing) combinations onto "lands on target with topspin" — the virtual-landing reward scores that manifold directly, so racket velocity becomes instrumental rather than terminal, exactly the degeneracy the vel gate has been fighting (memory: vel gate = composite bottleneck; 20k run degraded after ~10k). What it does **not** subsume: (a) the *approach* gradient — keep `racket_progress` + `racket_position`, since virtual terms are zero until the paddle is within 9.5 cm at the strike frame (the capture gate is a position gate at exactly today's acceptance scale, 7.5 cm); (b) early training — a from-scratch policy never enters the capture gate, so warm-start from the current E/A champion or keep vel/normal weights at 2.0 for the first ~2k iters then decay. Risks: single-frame evaluation at 50 Hz means ±10 ms timing jitter ≈ ±5 cm of paddle travel at 5 m/s — acceptable inside the 9.5 cm gate, but log the sensitivity; the reward is only as correct as the validated model (circularity handled in the ablation design below).

**Logging** (add to the command-term metrics block, pattern of lines 1076–1127): `virtual_hit_rate` (capture-gate pass per swing, EMA like `_exact_n_acc`), `virtual_net_clear_rate`, `virtual_land_inbounds_rate`, `virtual_land_err_mean/p90`, `virtual_topspin_revs_mean`, per-clip splits, plus `dbg_` raw-vs-gated kernels (existing `_dbg_log` machinery, hope_rewards.py:32–42). Sanity invariant: `virtual_hit_rate` should track `exact_strike_pos_success_10cm` (line 1121) closely.

**Falsifiable first ablation** (one 5090, ~2×2.8 h): baseline DeployParity stack vs +virtual terms (vel/normal at 0.5), 2000 it, 4096 envs, warm-start both from the same champion. Score BOTH policies' achieved exact-strike racket states **post-hoc through the same frozen model** (this makes the comparison fair, not circular — the model is the referee for both, and its own validity is established separately by the mocap-validation track). Falsifiable claim: *at equal budget the virtual-reward arm's landing-in-bounds-with-net-clearance rate ≥2× baseline and topspin ≥10 rev/s median, with `exact_strike_pos_success_10cm` regressing <10%*. If the landing rate doesn't move, the premise "landing signal is trainable through the contact model without a real ball" is falsified. Second-stage referee: MuJoCo C++ sim with the same `ball_physics.yaml` (single source of truth by construction) and a real served ball.

**Effort/risk:** ~2–4 days (physics files ported verbatim from the branch; new code = 1 sampler + 1 cached evaluation + 3 reward fns + metrics). Risk **low-medium**: no obs change, no PhysX change, deploy contract untouched; failure modes are reward-hacking (mitigations above are inherited from v0's own gates) and compute overhead (measured, bounded, compilable).

## TIER 2 — real simulated ball in Isaac (the v0 path, merged INTO the tracking task)

**What v0 already gives you** (F1): ball asset with neutralized PhysX restitution, 400 Hz aero callback (drag+Magnus as external force, `ball.py:55–89`), code-driven spin-equation table/paddle contacts with direct velocity+spin writes (`table_tennis_env.py:135–207`), serve event, hit-event one-shot semantics, landing/pass_net rewards on the *real* post-hit state, ball obs terms, terminations. **What it lacks**: motion imitation / racket-target integration (v0 is a bare joint-pos env), curriculum, deploy contract. Recommendation: graft ball+callback+serve into the tracking task env (subclass `ManagerBasedRLEnv` used there, register the callback the same way) rather than build up the bare v0 env — keeps imitation, footwork stack, and the trained champion as warm start; keep timing at the strike frame consistent (the serve must be phased so the ball reaches the strike zone at `time_to_strike≈0` — sample serve at swing resample, back-integrated from the commanded strike point, i.e. the Tier-1 sampler now *launches* the ball it previously imagined).

**Obs contract (breaks 175-D — new task name, do not mutate DeployParity):** actor += `ball_pos_b` (3) and a deploy-honest velocity: the relay streams ball **position only** at 300 Hz, so either (a) finite-difference/KF velocity computed identically in sim (with matching noise+delay DR) and in the C++ runner, or (b) a short position history (Ace uses ball pos+spin history; spin is off the table — no rigid body on the ball in matches). 175+6=181-D (or 175+3·H for history). **Spin, true velocity, predicted landing → critic only**, exactly as v0's CriticCfg already stages it (`table_tennis_env_cfg.py` PolicyCfg/CriticCfg; `observations.py:40–51`). Deploy chain impact: new ONNX obs layout, C++ obs-builder extension for VRPN ball position, new gate protocols — this is why Tier 1 ships first.

**Rewards:** replace `exact_strike` gating with the real `_hit_event` (consumed-once-per-step pattern, table_tennis_env.py:58–70); keep v0 `landing_in_opponent_half` 30 / `pass_net_margin` 20–50 with the net-clearance gate; add the Ace-style spin term on the real `ω_plus` at hit (same formula/weight as Tier 1); add a `ball_hit` one-shot bonus (~10) since hitting is no longer guaranteed by construction; retain the racket pos/progress terms as approach shaping toward the *predicted intercept point* (PACE's hit-guidance, §IV-B.1, with their clamp-inside-tolerance trick to avoid overfitting the intercept).

**Curriculum from served balls:** start at `ServeConfig.reachable_returner` (zero spin, narrow), widen pos/vel/spin boxes success-gated on `return_success_rate`, reusing the `ref_perturb` gating pattern (hope_commands.py cfg:1419–1423). Stage spin: 0 → ±60 → ±100 rad/s.

**Logging:** real `hit_rate`, `return_over_net_rate`, `landing_in_bounds_rate`, landing-point heatmap, `outgoing_spin_revs`, predicted-vs-actual landing error (the predictor and the live ball share one model — disagreement >few cm flags a callback bug), tunneling counter (ball passing paddle plane without event).

**Falsifiable first ablation:** Tier-1-trained policy evaluated zero-shot in the Tier-2 env (frozen): if the virtual-ball training transfers, its real return rate on `reachable_returner` serves should be ≫ the pre-Tier-1 champion's (which never saw a landing objective). This is also the cleanest test of the whole "reward through the model" premise before paying the obs-contract cost.

**Effort/risk:** ~1–2 weeks + retraining + deploy-chain work. Risk **medium-high**: per-substep callback correctness at 4096 envs (v0's subset `write_root_velocity_to_sim`, table_tennis_env.py:209–215, is untested at scale), 400 Hz physics + CCD roughly doubles physics cost vs the tracking task's 200 Hz (sim.dt 0.0025 vs 0.005 — expect ≥+50% iter time, needs measurement), contact-sphere hit detection is approximate (blade is a disc, not a sphere), and the 181-D contract forks the deploy path.

## TIER 3 — rally / win-the-point (sketch only)
Prereq: Tier 2 validated + venue-validated ball model. Shape per Ace: (i) episodes = points, terminal-state rewards (miss / hit-no-return / return / opponent-unreturnable), keeping Tier-2 dense terms as shaping at decayed weight; (ii) opponent = distribution over incoming balls conditioned on our previous shot — first a *scripted returner* driven by the same flight+contact model fit to venue opponents' tendencies, then league self-play (two policy instances across the table, ball state handed between them); (iii) skill conditioning à la Ace (`ydesired`, `wreward=[wp, ws]`) as commanded inputs so match-play strategy (aim + spin selection) is chosen at deploy time by a planner rather than baked in — note this adds conditioning obs (contract change again); (iv) our on-policy PPO lacks Ace's replay-based tricks (event tables, HER) — nearest equivalents: prioritized env resets from post-swing states (the A8 buffer already on `p2-multiswing`) and curriculum over opponent difficulty. Effort: months; do not start before Tier 2's zero-shot ablation passes.
